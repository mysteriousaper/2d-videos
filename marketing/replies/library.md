# WiyMood reply library (comments and Direct) · DRAFT for team approval, 15 Nov 2026

**Never** end a message with «Հաջողություն» (team lead, 15 Nov 2026: sounds rude and out of place in Armenian, like a brush-off). End with an offer to help instead: «Հարցեր լինեն, գրեք, սիրով կօգնենք 🙌».

**Rule:** people only ever get texts from this file. The bot and Claude pick a variant (at random, or the one that fits);
nobody generates new Armenian for a person. No names are inserted (wrong declension risk). A situation that isn't
here goes to the team in Telegram; once the team writes an answer, it is added here.

**Phase now: ACTIVITY** (team lead, 15 Nov 2026). We want comments, saves and shares, not calls or meetings.
We don't push anyone to call, we don't chase, we don't follow up when someone goes quiet.
Prices only in Direct and only if asked. Public replies: lowercase «դուք/ձեր».

## C. The free check (sent in Direct)

### C0. Rules for every check (team lead, 15 Nov 2026, learned on the first real one)
1. **Watch the real work first.** Screen recording or screenshots of the posts with their numbers. Never praise or judge what we haven't seen.
2. **Open with one concrete fact from their page:** a named post and its real numbers (likes, shares, comments). No generic compliments («մոնտաժ անել գիտեք»): they read as box-ticking. The opening must lead straight into point 1, not hang on its own.
3. **One "why":** say why their strong thing works (audience, emotion, format). That's what makes it sound like an expert, not a checklist.
4. **One growth step:** what to do next (a series, a format, a rhythm), not only what to fix.
5. **Generic advice last or out** (watermarks, before/after, bio). Anyone can read it in a blog.
6. **Short:** at most 4 short paragraphs, readable on a phone.
7. **Gentle:** "keep it separate", never "stop doing what you like".
8. **First fix takes minutes** and they can do it themselves.
9. **Numbers only as they appear on the page;** never estimate or round up.
10. **Close with an offer to help** («Հարցեր լինեն, գրեք, սիրով կօգնենք 🙌»), never «Հաջողություն», never a call.
11. Every new sentence goes through `marketing/hy_check.py` and the team's eyes before it is sent; once used, it is added here.

Frame:
> Նայեցինք ձեր էջը։ [one fact from their page + why it works]։ Ահա 3 բան, որ կօգնի․
> 1. …
> 2. …
> 3. …
> [the 2-minute step]։ Հարցեր լինեն, գրեք, սիրով կօգնենք 🙌

If the page is already good, the first line is:
> Նայեցինք ձեր էջը, լավ վիճակում է 👍 Ահա 3 մանր բան, որ դեռ կարելի է բարելավել․
