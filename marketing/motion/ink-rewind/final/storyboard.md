# «Մի հատ նորից» · ink version · storyboard

One idea: the whole story is drawn live by an invisible pen in one long sketchbook. The camera glides across a single sheet from drawing to drawing (one continuous world, no cuts). At «Մի րոպե՛» the pen stops; on «Մի հատ նորից» the drawing *un-draws* itself backwards (ink pulled back into the pen) and the camera flies back to the start, then the good version is drawn in blue ink.

Style: black ink on warm paper, outlines only (no fills, no hatching), lines boil at 10 fps, a loose second line in places, small breaks. Red ink = loss, blue ink = fixed. Captions: solid hand-lettered ink at the bottom, inside the safe zone (x 110–970, y 1440–1600).

Camera: world positions zig-zag; 0.45 s heavy-eased glide between drawings with a slight tilt; slow push and drift inside each drawing. Depth: far pencil doodles (0.55 parallax), paper (1.0), dust specks foreground (1.25).
