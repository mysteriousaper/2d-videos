# «Գինը՝ Direct-ով» — ink reel plan (draft for the team's pick)

**One idea:** when the price is hidden, buyers don't ask; they guess high and leave, and the owner never knows.
**Who:** owners who sell standard products or services on Instagram (shop, bakery, salon, café).
**POV:** the buyer, like «Մի հատ նորից».
**Job:** problem post. Next step: a send ("send this to a business that still writes…"), with no "write us" (activity phase).
**Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 40 s (≈ 90 spoken words), our ink engine, one continuous camera over one sheet.
**Thread through the whole reel:** one red price tag on a string. It starts empty with a «?», fills with the buyer's growing guesses, gets torn off, and at the turn the owner's pen writes the real price on it. It ends hanging under the logo, filled in.

## Easter eggs (big enough to read on a phone)
1. A shop sign: «Գինը՝ ըստ տրամադրության» ("price depends on the mood"), a wink at Wiy**Mood**.
2. The DM draft counter: «Սևագիր (3)», because the buyer deleted it three times.
3. Wiy hidden inside one thought bubble, shrugging.
4. The competitor's shop name: «Գինը գրված է» ("The price is written").
5. In the owner's chat list, the only message: «Սպամ՝ «Շահել եք iPhone»» (spam: "You won an iPhone").

## Rules check
- No statistics on screen. The research numbers (85 % leave, etc.) come from old or weak sources, and house rules say never invent or overstate. The reel runs on the psychology (guessing high, the cost of asking); the sources can go in the caption if the team wants.
- Prices in the reel are the buyer's imagined guesses and a sample competitor price, not WiyMood prices.
- Text inside the safe zone (y 270–1248, 65 px sides); cover inside the 3:4 band (y 240–1680).
- Different music from «Մի հատ նորից»; DnB/jungle is our signature for story reels.
