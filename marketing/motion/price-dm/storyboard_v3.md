# «Տասից՝ զրո»: a website that sells and has taste (v3 draft for review)

**Idea:** a website loses buyers at concrete points, and the owner blames the wrong thing. A good site does two jobs at once: it draws people in with taste and sells with simplicity. WiyMood builds those.
**Voice:** WiyMood, speaking to the owner («դուք»), calm and sure; about 38 s spoken + 2 s ending.
**World:** one big drawn sheet, one continuous camera. The "bad site" is drawn as a real place: a corridor of 5 rooms that buyers walk through (2.5D, depth, shadows). The "good site" is drawn by Wiy and fills with colour.

## Characters
| Who | Look and personality |
|---|---|
| **Մարի** (the buyer we follow) | Red scarf, phone in one hand and a coffee in the other, quick and decisive; she really wants the cake |
| **Արամ** (the owner) | Baker, flour on a blue apron, a big moustache, proud of his cakes; sits by the phone waiting for orders |
| **9 other buyers**, each different | A grandpa with a cane, a student in headphones, a mum with a pram, a man in a tie checking his watch, a girl walking a dog, a teen on a skateboard, a grandma with thick glasses, a cyclist in a helmet, a couple holding hands |
| **Wiy** (our mascot) | Appears at the turn, builds the good site with a giant pen |

## Notes
- "10 buyers" is a story picture, not a statistic; no numbers are claimed as data.
- Prices of WiyMood services are not shown (house rule).
- Captions: design C (sketch frame).
- Easter eggs (readable on a phone): the 2012 room's hit counter «Դուք 000007-րդ այցելուն եք», a Comic Sans «Բարի գալուստ!!!», a cat sleeping on the PDF, Wiy hidden in the crowd of buyers in line 1, «Wi-Fi»-shaped cobweb.
