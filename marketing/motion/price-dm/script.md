## How to record
- Quiet room with soft things around; no fan or AC.
- Phone voice memo or USB mic, 15–20 cm from the mouth, slightly to the side.
- WAV if possible (m4a is fine). 2 s of silence at the start.
- Read the whole script 2–3 times in one file, with ~0.5 s between lines. I'll pick the best take of each line and time every picture to your words.
