## How to record
- Quiet room with soft things around (curtains, sofa, wardrobe); no fan, no AC.
- Phone voice memo or the USB mic, 15–20 cm from the mouth, slightly to the side.
- WAV if possible (m4a is fine too). Keep 2 s of silence at the start.
- Read the whole script 2–3 times in one file; I'll pick the best take of each line.
- A pause of ~0.5 s between lines makes the timing easy; the animation follows your voice, so don't rush.
